Kedmanee layout?

Everything in this app assumes the Kedmanee layout. Thai has several keyboard layouts, and Kedmanee is by far the most widely used. The name is a Thai surname: it honours Suwanprasert Kedmanee (สุวรรณประเสริฐ เกษมณี), a Thai employee of the Remington typewriter company who refined the layout in the 1930s. Frequent letters sit on the unshifted keys and rare ones behind Shift, and the layout is codified as Thai Industrial Standard TIS 820. (A more efficient rival, the Pattachote layout, never caught on.) The standard Thai keyboards on Windows, macOS and iPhone — and the keycaps printed on keyboards sold in Thailand — are all Kedmanee, so the finger memory you build here works on every device.
